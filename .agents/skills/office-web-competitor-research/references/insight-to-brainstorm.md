# Evidence to ideas

Create evidence cards: task/context, observed event, consequence, source/run ID, evidence type, confidence and counterevidence. Separate product failure from proven user pain.

Build opportunity statements: For [user in task], [obstacle] prevents [desired outcome], evidenced by [runs/users]. State whether frequency/cost is measured or unknown.

For each idea record: target job, web-specific benefit, why PPT/image/current tool is insufficient, entry mode, source assets, acceptance bar, Baidu asset hypothesis, competing alternative, hardest assumption, cheapest discriminating experiment and stop criterion.

Brainstorm across alternatives: improve attachment interpretation; improve clarification; template selection; preview/recipient experience; correction and maintenance. Do not assume all problems need a new agent or webpage.

Prioritize with explicit ordinal judgments and confidence: pain, recurrence, web-fit, differentiation, reach, feasibility, willingness to pay. Do not multiply unknown quantities. Ship no “market gap” claim from a small convenience sample without qualification.
